## Specific Sub-Issues

1. **Provider/city mismatch coverage gap** — Geocode provider fails on addresses where the entered city name doesn't match provider expectations (e.g., unincorporated areas, city/county boundary ambiguity)
2. **Missing city-stripping retry in edge function** — When initial geocode fails, no fallback logic strips the city name and retries with just street + state/zip
3. **Missing user-facing feedback on final geocode failure** — When all retries fail, designer sees no error, no warning, just silently missing downstream data
