# AI Futurists to Follow: The Thinker, The Builder, The Visionary

**TO:** The Family

**DATE:** 2026-02-23  
**SUBJECT:** Three "Patron Saints" to Guide Our Hunt

This is primer fuel. A quick-read guide to three essential voices in AI whose thinking should directly influence our mission. They provide a balanced perspective: the societal philosopher, the technical builder, and the exponential visionary.

---

### 1. David Shapiro: The Societal Philosopher
*   **Who & Why:** Futurist and AI communicator focused on "Post-Labor Economics." He matters because he forces us to think about the end-game of cognitive automation and its impact on society.
*   **X/Twitter:** [`@DaveShapi`](https://x.com/DaveShapi)
*   **Current Focus:** The "white-collar bloodbath"—the imminent, large-scale automation of cognitive jobs. He is focused on what new economic and social structures (like UBI) will be necessary when knowledge work is no longer a human bottleneck.
*   **The ONE Idea to Internalize:** **Cognitive Automation is Imminent.** Repetitive mental tasks are going the way of the factory assembly line. We must design our systems not just to *assist* human analysts, but to be precursors to a fully autonomous "cognitive assembly line" for discovering insights.
*   **Connection to Our Mission:** His work pushes us to build a platform that targets patterns currently undiscoverable due to the limits of human attention and time. The goal is a system that automates the process of inquiry itself.

---

### 2. Jim Fan: The Technical Builder
*   **Who & Why:** Senior Research Scientist at NVIDIA, leading their AI Agents Initiative. He is on the front lines of building "Generally Capable Agents" that can operate in both virtual and physical worlds. He shows us what's happening at the cutting edge of agent development.
*   **X/Twitter:** [`@DrJimFan`](https://x.com/DrJimFan)
*   **Current Focus:** "Embodied AI" and creating a "Foundation Agent" (Project GR00T) that can learn and act across diverse, complex environments like simulations, games, and robotics.
*   **The ONE Idea to Internalize:** **The World is the Best Teacher.** The next leap in AI won't come from just bigger models, but from agents that can *act* and *learn* within rich, interactive environments. Intelligence is forged through action and consequence.
*   **Connection to Our Mission:** His thinking directly informs our goal of building a multi-agent system. Our platform should be less of a static analysis tool and more of a *simulation environment* where agents can be unleashed on data to form, test, and validate hypotheses automatically. Pattern discovery becomes an active, not a passive, process.

---

### 3. Peter Diamandis: The Exponential Visionary
*   **Who & Why:** Entrepreneur, founder of XPRIZE and Singularity University. He is a master of the "abundance" and "moonshot" mindset, framing technology as the engine for solving humanity's grandest challenges.
*   **X/Twitter:** [`@PeterDiamandis`](https://x.com/PeterDiamandis)
*   **Current Focus:** The *convergence* of multiple exponential technologies (AI, robotics, energy, biotech, space) as the primary accelerator of progress. He's talking about audacious goals like lunar data centers and leveraging AI for radical longevity.
*   **The ONE Idea to Internalize:** **Convergence is the Accelerator.** The most powerful breakthroughs happen at the intersection of different fields. AI is a catalyst, but its true power is unleashed when combined with other exponential trends.
*   **Connection to Our Mission:** His work challenges us to build a "convergence engine." The platform shouldn't just find patterns within a single dataset; it should be designed to ingest and correlate data from radically different domains (e.g., genomic data + climate models + financial data) to find the non-obvious patterns that exist *between* them.
