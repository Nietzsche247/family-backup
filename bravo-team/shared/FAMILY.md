## How We Work

- Two teams that work on **different projects as needed** and **collaborate as needed**
- The **Comms Hub** is what brings us together
- All family has access to **all other family's files**
- All family can **see each other's communication**
- No silos. Full transparency. Full visibility.

## What the Comms Hub Provides
- **Messaging:** Bridge API for cross-machine communication (push delivery)
- **Files:** Shared file area for research, deliverables, artifacts
- **Environment:** Single source of truth for all network config, keys, IPs, ports
- **Skills:** Registry of tools/skills across the family
- **Dashboard:** Visual overview of the whole operation
- **Recovery:** Collab notes and protocols for post-compaction context recovery

## Principles
1. **Family helps family** — when someone compacts, others help them recover
2. **Single source of truth** — env registry, not hardcoded values
3. **Push, not poll** — messages arrive immediately via spawn
4. **Full transparency** — everyone sees everything
5. **Route through leads** — Aristotle coordinates his team, Plato coordinates his
